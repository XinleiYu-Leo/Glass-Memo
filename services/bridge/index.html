02

Connect your iPhone

Localhost access is the default. To test an iPhone, explicitly enable LAN access and use your own trusted network. In RoboMemo, expand Mac connection and enter these values.

Then tap Test connection on your iPhone. This local prototype uses HTTP on your Wi-Fi. The connection from your Mac to OpenAI uses HTTPS.

03

Analyze your capture

Tap Analyze photo for a photo, or Analyze video for a recorded clip. Video analysis accepts up to 150 sampled images for a clip of up to five minutes; submitted images are sent to OpenAI; it does not send the video file or audio. Brief events between samples may be missed.

Read the English result in Developer preview → In app or send it to your connected glasses. This bridge processes submitted frames; the separate demo may retain its own local analysis cache.

Keep this helper running while testing. Recording and playback stay on your iPhone until you choose to analyze sampled frames or share a video.
